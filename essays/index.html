Machines of Loving Grace, Two Years On

In 2024, Dario Amodei published Machines of Loving Grace, an essay about what powerful AI could do for the world if things go well. Reading it in 2026 means reading a prediction document while some of its bets are already being scored. This series grades it section by section, from 25 years of building conversational and agentic AI systems in production.

  • Where Brute Force Ends

    September 15, 2026 · The essay's engine is a country of geniuses in a datacenter compressing a century of science into a decade. Brute force can search any haystack, but only after someone defines the needle.

  • The Dataset Problem

    September 15, 2026 · The economics section asks the developing world to generalize from five confounded examples in a game that optimizes back. Development is a dataset problem, and the dataset is adversarial.
